Why MNTN for a brokerage like The Agency

The Agency already knows how to sell the dream on Instagram and in glossy print. What it does not have is the reach of television with the accountability of performance marketing. That is the opening.

MNTN puts The Agency's brand on premium, full-screen connected TV, the streaming apps its affluent audience actually watches, and then does the thing linear never could: it ties every impression back to a real outcome on theagencyre.com. This is not a branding buy. It is measurable pipeline that happens to run through the most premium screen in the house.

For the brand itself, CTV puts The Agency in the living rooms of high net worth households in the exact markets that matter, Beverly Hills, Aspen, Miami, the Hamptons, at a scale and polish that reinforces the luxury positioning rather than cheapening it. Household targeting concentrates spend on the most affluent zip codes and the specific metros where The Agency has offices and inventory, instead of a wasteful national spray.

That same targeting drives traffic. Someone sees the spot, and MNTN moves them to the site, to a specific listing, to an office page, or to an agent profile, and proves it happened. The retargeting layer is where the numbers get interesting: luxury has a long consideration window, so the buyers and sellers browsing eight figure listings this month are the highest intent audience The Agency owns. MNTN keeps The Agency in front of them across the months a transaction takes to mature, and it works both sides of the marketplace, driving seller leads and home valuation and list-with-us flows on the sell side, and agent recruiting on the other.

The reason this beats the current mix is measurement. Performance TV reports the metrics a CMO and a COO will defend in a board meeting: site visits driven by CTV, conversions, and cost per outcome by market. That turns TV from a branding line item into a performance channel worth scaling. And scaling is the natural close: prove it in two or three flagship metros, then roll the identical, measurable playbook out office by office across the network. The Agency's franchise model and MNTN's market by market scalability are a clean fit.
